Biography

I am a Research Intern at Microsoft Research India, working on generative and representational vision models. I defended my Ph.D. thesis in July 2026 at IIT Madras.

My research focuses on diffusion models, self-supervised learning, representation learning, and multi-modal approaches for medical imaging.

I have published at MICCAI, MIDL, IEEE ISBI, IEEE JBHI, and Elsevier CMPB, with research experience across IIT Madras, GE Healthcare, and NuvoAI.
