Biography

I am a Research Intern at Microsoft Research India and recently defended my Ph.D. thesis (July 2026) at IIT Madras, where I built generative and representational vision models for medical imaging.

My research centres on diffusion models, self-supervised and representation learning, and multi-modal methods — with applications spanning brain MRI, histology reconstruction, and image restoration.
